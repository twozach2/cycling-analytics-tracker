The route-map thumbnails in this directory were downloaded from Zwift's
official cycling-route support catalogs on 2026-08-07. The catalog directory is:

https://support.zwift.com/zwift-worlds-and-cycling-routes-rk3PMBUht

The included maps cover Watopia and every scheduled guest world: France,
Innsbruck, London, Makuri Islands, New York, Paris, Richmond, Scotland, and
Yorkshire.

Daily guest-world availability is read from the Zwift Insider world calendar:

https://zwiftinsider.com/schedule/
